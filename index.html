05

Open source. Built on GitHub.

The VIN decoder, recalls and complaints run on free public NHTSA APIs today. Maintenance schedules and parts pricing plug in through provider adapters.

Decode

NHTSA vPIC

Free, no key. Year, make, model, trim, engine, transmission and plant from the VIN.

Safety

NHTSA recalls

Free, no key. Recalls and owner complaints by year, make and model.

Maintenance

CarMD or similar

Paid key. OEM service schedule by mileage, plus repair costs and check-engine code lookups.

Parts

eBay Browse API

Free developer key. Live prices with fitment checks for your vehicle, sorted cheapest first.
