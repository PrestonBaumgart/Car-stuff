## Engine parts

Detailed engine models (engine/), fitted to the code-built parts when the engine bay or the inside of the engine is
opened. All are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The pistons, rods and valves were cut out of a full V6, and the crankshaft out of a four-cylinder assembly. The scanned
parts were simplified, and every model was turned and scaled to a common frame and recolored; the starter's photo texture
was baked into vertex colors.
